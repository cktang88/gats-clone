# Zombies

Zombies is co-op base defense for a private squad of up to four, with bots in the empty seats. The squad holds the core, presented as the Bastion with 50 survivors inside, at the center of the Outpost map through a 40-second day of building walls and turrets and repairing them, then a night whose horde walks in from the edges the night table names. Later nights send their packs in waves. A night ends once its whole horde has spawned and died, or at first light, 90s after its last pack walks in, which burns any stragglers. Ten nights grow harder, the horde splits across sides from night 3, night 5 and night 8 bring a Colossus and night 10, the Tide, brings two and everything from every side; holding through the Tide wins the run, and the core's fall or the loss of its last survivor loses it. Either ends the run with a report; 20 seconds later a fresh run starts. A player whose health runs out goes down, can be revived by a squadmate holding use for 3 seconds, and otherwise bleeds out after 25 seconds; by night the Bastion sends them back 15 seconds later for a few survivors, else they return at dawn. A human who joins during the night sits out until dawn. Walkers and plated turn on a living squad player within 160px in plain sight, runners within 360px; brutes, bloaters and the Colossus ignore the squad and march on the core, which shrugs off 40% of each bite and can be repaired. Every 100 hp of harm the core takes costs a survivor, mended or not. Plating takes 10 off every bullet, down to 1, unless the round pierces; blasts get through whole. A bloater bursts where it dies, hurting squad, horde, every building within 110px and the core if it is that close. Sentries, cannons, scatters and mortars fire real rounds at the horde on ammo bought from the shared scrap bank, tesla coils arc lightning through it, ammo depots and repair posts tend the squad's guns, walls and people, spike strips slow the horde on the floor, and the Bastion's survivors fire from its walls. Walls come in three tiers (barricade, sandbag, steel) and walls, turrets, depots and posts upgrade in place to level 3. The browser client starts and joins squads from the menu, draws the horde, the core, the walls and the turrets, and has a build mode, a run HUD, callouts, sounds and the report.

## Sub-features

- `zom-squad-room` opens with `POST /api/squads`, which answers `{ room: 'z-' + 6 base-32 characters }`. `GET /api/servers` never lists it. One address may open `squadsPerMin` (6) a minute (429 after), at most `squadRooms` (20) run at once (503 after), and a room with no humans for `squadIdleMs` (30s) closes and its code stops answering the upgrade. That 30s is wall-clock time. `LIMITS` in `src/server/limits.ts` holds all three.
- `zom-standstill` freezes a squad room while no human is joined: the day clock, the night, bleed-outs and the restart countdown all wait. A squad nobody has joined yet sits on day 1 with 40s left, and a human who rejoins within the 30s idle window picks up where the room stopped.
- `zom-seats` keeps four players: bots fill the seats humans leave, a joining human takes a bot's seat, and a fifth human gets `{t:'error', message:'Room full'}`. Everyone is on team `red`, so friendly fire never applies; in a run only zombie bites hurt the squad, never its own blasts.
- `zom-run` is the state machine in `src/shared/sim/run.ts`: `day` (until `phaseEndsAt`, or sooner once every living human sent `{t:'ready'}`; `ready` lists their ids, bots count as ready but a squad of bots alone waits out the day), `night` (`phaseEndsAt` null until the last pack walks in, then first light, when stragglers burn for no scrap and dawn comes; `waveLeft` counts zombies alive or to come), `over` (`phaseEndsAt` is the restart time and `report` holds night reached, `won` (true after the Tide's dawn), `survivors`, `durationMs`, a row of kills, revives and buildings built for every player in the squad when the core fell and for anyone who left after scoring one of those, `turretKills` by turret kind and `bastionKills`). The snapshot's `run` field carries it with `scrap`, `core` (`x`, `y`, `hp`, `maxHp`), `aliveZombies`, `survivors` and `lost` (tonight's, or last night's by day). `NIGHTS` in `src/shared/defs.ts` is the night table: one row per night with each kind's count for four bots (a human counts one and a half, every listed kind sends at least one, and a boss, a kind with `pack: 1`, comes as listed for any squad with its health scaled by the squad's share; `hordeCount` in `defs.ts` holds the rule), the sides it comes from (`north`, `east`, `south`, `west`), and a `name` on the boss nights. The horde spawns as packs (`ZOMBIES[kind].pack`) from those sides only, `ZOM.packsPerWave(night)` (night − 1) at a time, each wave followed by that many `packGapMs`. Every `ZOM.survivorHp` (100) of harm the core takes costs a survivor through `hurtCore()` in `horde.ts`, whatever is mended between, and losing the last ends the run; each one left pays 3 scrap at dawn. A fallen core takes everyone left inside.
- `zom-horde` sends each player the zombies in view as tuples `[id, kindIndex, x, y, hpTenths]` in `snap.zombies` (`kindIndex` into `ZOMBIE_KINDS`: 0 walker, 1 brute, 2 runner, 3 plated, 4 bloater, 5 colossus). A zombie death ships a `zkill` event whose `by` is the player whose own shot, blade or blast killed it, null for a turret's, the Bastion's or first light's; each player sees `dmg` events (kind `zombie`) only for their own hits, one per zombie per tick. A blast, a turret round or a Bastion round sends no per-zombie `dmg`. `hpTenths` and a building's `hp` and `ammo` read 10 only when whole, so anything short of whole reads 9 or less.
- `zom-walls` takes `{t:'build', kind, cx, cy, lv?}` (`kind` one of `BUILDING_KINDS`: `wall`, `sentry`, `cannon`, `scatter`, `mortar`, `tesla`, `depot`, `post`, `spikes`; `lv` 1 to 3 is a wall's tier and defaults to 1), `{t:'upgrade', cx, cy}` and `{t:'demolish', cx, cy}` on the 50px grid (`ZOM.cell`). `buildRefusal()` in `src/shared/sim/build.ts` holds the rules, and both the server and the client's build preview call it. A cell with a building on it reads `taken` before anything else, so it can come down with someone pressed against it. `snap.buildings` lists `{kind, cx, cy, hp}` (hp in tenths of the building's full health at its level) plus `lv` (2 or 3, absent at level 1: a wall's tier, a turret's or utility's level) and `ammo` (tenths of a full load at its level, 0 once it cannot fire) on a turret, and is sticky: the wire omits it while unchanged. Spike strips are in the list too but are floor (`World.floor`), not on the horde's flow field and not solid. Buildings block bodies but not the squad's bullets or grenades. Costs and base health live in `BUILDINGS` in `src/shared/defs.ts`, the three wall tiers in `WALL_TIERS`, level scaling in `UPGRADE` and utility numbers in `UTILITY`.
- `zom-tiers` are the three walls in `WALL_TIERS`: barricade 10 scrap and 800 hp, sandbag wall 24 and 2000, steel wall 60 and 4800. A wall's `armor` is the share of each bite it turns aside (0, 10%, 30%), applied after the horde's tick by `tickArmor()` in `src/shared/sim/utility.ts` from the hp it held before it; its `blast` scales a bloater's burst on it (1, 0.75, 0.5); its `repairMul` is how much faster holding use mends it (1.5, 1, 0.8). Taking one down pays `floor(invested * demolishRefund * hp/10)`, `investedOf()` in `build.ts` being every price paid so far, upgrades included.
- `zom-upgrade` is `upgrade()` in `src/shared/sim/run.ts` for `{t:'upgrade', cx, cy}`, judged by `upgradeRefusal()` (`notDay`, `outOfReach`, `none`, `maxed`, `scrap`) that the client's hover calls too. A wall pays the difference of its tiers' prices (14 then 36), anything else `UPGRADE.costShare` (65%, then 115%) of its base price; it keeps its share of health and load. `turretDef(kind, lv)` gives a turret's stats at a level (`UPGRADE`: damage 1, 1.4, 1.9; fire gap 1, 0.85, 0.7; range 1, 1.1, 1.2; load 1, 1.5, 2.2; a mortar's blast damage with it), `maxHpOf()` health (1, 1.4, 1.9), `auraOf()` a depot's and a post's work rate (1, 1.6, 2.4) and `reachAt()` their reach.
- `zom-utility` is the tesla coil, ammo depot, repair post and spike strip. The coil is a turret (`TURRET_KINDS` has `tesla`, its def has an `arc`): `zap()` in `turrets.ts` hits its target for `damage`, then the nearest zombie within `arc.reach` of the last, `arc.jumps` times, `falloff` weaker each, through cover and plating, and emits `{e:'coil', x, y, p}` (the arc's points) instead of a `turret` event and a bullet. Depots and posts are solid buildings handled by `tickUtilities()` (a depot refills the neediest turret in reach and a squad player's gun, a post mends players and buildings, each saying so at most once a second in `{e:'aid', kind, x, y}`); spike strips are floor, slowed and hurt and worn by `tickTraps()` around the horde's tick, and `spikeSlowAt()` is the hook a horde AI can read instead.
- `zom-turrets` is `tickTurrets()` in `src/shared/sim/turrets.ts`. A loaded turret whose gun has cooled fires one real bullet (`Bullet.turret` set, owner its builder) at the nearest zombie in range of the kind it `prefers` (sentry walkers, cannon brutes, scatter runners, mortar plated), else the nearest of any kind, skipping any that a map wall or crate hides unless it lobs, and any whose `plate` would eat over half its round (so sentries and scatters leave plated and the Colossus alone). Squad buildings never block any turret's view: the mortar's edge is splash on packs, lobbed over map cover. A scatter fires `pellets` (7) rounds a shot over 260px. A mortar's shell flies over everything to where its target will be when it lands, from the zombie's last-tick velocity (`Zombie.vx`, `vy`), and bursts there (`Bullet.lobbed`, a 120px blast that ignores plating); its `turret` event carries `reach`. `node scripts/mortar-aim.ts` measures the share of each blast that lands on a lone zombie marching in. The Bastion fires `BASTION_GUN` rounds from the core's center at zombies within 220px, on its own clock (`run.bastionFireAt`), once per 300ms with all 50 survivors and slower as they fall; they are rounds on `snap.bullets` with no owner and `turret: 'bastion'`, and their kills add to `run.bastionKills`. Each shot spends one ammo and ships `{e:'turret', kind, x, y, angle}` (cell center, angle to 0.01 rad). Turret rounds stay off `snap.bullets`; the client flies each from its event to the first zombie on its line. A turret's kill, a mortar's blast included, pays the bank, scores for its builder while they are in the room, and adds to `run.turretKills[kind][zombieKind]`, never to anyone's `kills`.
- `zom-downed` shows a downed player in `players` with `downed: { revive (0..1), bleedOutAt (server time) }`; `alive` is false. `life` events report `downed`, `revived` (with `by`) and `bledOut`. A bled-out player's `respawn` message does nothing; by night the Bastion sends them back after `ZOM.reinforce.ms` (15s) for `ZOM.reinforce.survivors(night)` survivors (1 plus half the night, rounded up) while more than that are left, else dawn brings them back. A human whose `join` lands during the night gets the same dead state, so leaving and rejoining never skips going down.
- `zom-use` is the `use` flag on `InputState`, bound to E: held within 70px of a downed squadmate it revives. Otherwise it serves what `serviceTarget()` in `src/shared/sim/build.ts` names, the rule the client's hint also uses: the nearest damaged building, worn core or turret short of ammo within 250px (of the cell center or the core's center), for scrap, day or night. A building costs `repairScrapPerHp` (half its price for the share worn, so mending beats tearing down and rebuilding), the core 1 per 5 hp, and `scrapPerRound` per round reloaded, a full load in `ZOM.refillMs` (2.5s). A worn turret is repaired before it is reloaded. Squad bots run errands, first match wins: revive a downed squadmate (a human before a bot, horde or not); reload a turret at 2 tenths or less unless a zombie is within 140px; while no zombie in sight is within 300px, mend a worn building or reload a turret within 550px of the core, the one nearest the bot's post; by day in a squad with no other human, do the next step of `nextBuildStep()` in `src/server/bot/siege.ts` once the bank can pay, from a spot two cells to its side: the first nine turrets of `BASTION_PLAN` (offsets count from the core's edge cells, so the ring stands 175px out on every side), the turret nearest the side tonight's `NIGHTS` row comes from to level 2, the last three turrets, an ammo depot and a repair post beside that side's guns, four sandbag walls across its way, level 2 for every turret (that side first), four spike strips beyond the walls, a tesla coil, the walls to steel, and level 3 for every turret; then mend the core while the bank has scrap beyond that building's cost; else hold a post 320px out from the core on a bearing set by the bot's id, or just inside the first squad wall on that bearing. A zombie within 140px makes a posted bot back off or sidestep while staying inside 550px. They shoot the nearest zombie in sight throughout, and walk a path on the bot nav grid with the core and squad buildings closed. With a human in the squad the bank is theirs: bots build nothing, keep `HUMANS_RESERVE` (the dearest building's cost, the tesla coil's 260) in the bank, mend and reload above it only, refill dry turrets and mend a core under half at night regardless.
- `zom-menu` is the Zombies block under the room list (`#squad`). `#squad-start` posts `/api/squads` and joins at once, and puts `?squad=<code>` in the address bar. A page opened with `?squad=<code>` lists the squad as `#squad-room` (selected), shows `#squad-link` with `#squad-copy`, and scrolls to it once the rooms load. A malformed code shows `That invite link is broken…` in `#menu-status` and drops the code from the address bar; a well-formed code whose room is gone shows `Squad <code> has closed. Start a new one.` and drops the code from the address bar. In game, `#squad-chip` names the squad with a copy button.
- `zom-world` draws walkers (small green, two arms), brutes (large purple, shoulder pads, red eyes, an hp bar), runners (smallest, sand), plated (steel grey with a thick ink rim and yellow eyes), bloaters (large orange) and the Colossus (huge dark red, shoulder pads, yellow eyes, an hp bar) as flat discs with an ink rim, with batched arms and eyes per kind, with a white flash on your own hits and a green splat on each `zkill`. The core is a tall dark block with a cyan crystal that turns red at 35% health or less, an hp ring and a white flash when it loses health. Walls are brick-coursed blocks that darken and crack by hp and flash when bitten. Every building stands in the same fixed tilt as the map, top face on its cell and shadow down and to the right. A turret is a low concrete pad under a flat dome (grey for a sentry, brown for a cannon, teal-grey for a scatter, olive for a mortar) with a barrel (twin thin barrels on a sentry, one thick one on a cannon, a flared mouth on a scatter, a short fat tube on a mortar) and an accent dot on the dome (yellow on a sentry, red on a cannon, teal on a scatter, violet on a mortar), that eases toward each shot's angle and recoils, and faces away from the core until it first fires. It has a muzzle flash, an ammo bar along its bottom edge that blinks red when empty, and the walls' cracks and bite flash. Downed squadmates lie flat and squashed with a white cross, a revive ring and a bleed-out clock. Squadmates wear their own colors, since the squad shares one team. At night the floor, walls, buildings, the core and cracks darken toward a cool blue over 1.5s while rounds, bodies and effects drawn after the wash stay bright, and the HUD's text drawn straight on the world turns light.
- `zom-build` is build mode: B toggles it by day (at night the chat says `You build by day.`), and nightfall or going down turns it off. 1 to 9 pick wall, sentry, cannon, scatter, mortar, tesla, depot, post and spikes (`buildKindForKey`); the wall key again, Q, or the mouse wheel steps the wall's tier (the wheel steps through every item, walls by tier first, `stepItem`), and a click or tap on a chip picks it; the picked chip is gold. The build bar's rows from the top are `WALLS` (`I Barricade 10`, `II Sandbag wall 24`, `III Steel wall 60`), `TURRETS` (keys 2 to 6) and `UTILITY` (7 to 9), then `BUILD` with the clicks, `U upgrade`, `1 · Q · wheel wall tier` and B. While build mode is on, the number keys pick kinds, never perks, and the perk dock waits. A ghost of the picked kind and tier follows the cursor, drawn through: green with `Sandbag wall · 24 scrap`, red with the refusal otherwise (`Cannon needs 180 scrap` when the bank is short). Over a standing building it is orange, with the plate `Sentry II · level 2/3 · health 100%` and under it `U or click: upgrade to Sentry III · 81 scrap · Right click: take down +58` (or `Upgrade to X needs N scrap`, `Fully upgraded`, `No upgrades`), and the bar's upgrade chip lights `upgrade to Sentry III 81`. U, that chip (the cursor may rest on it: the hovered cell stays judged), or a left click on the building upgrades it; outside build mode U upgrades the nearest building in reach and a gold `U to upgrade the wall to Sandbag wall · 14 scrap` hint shows by day when the bank can pay. Dashed rings show the build radius around the core and your reach. Left click builds, right click takes down; neither fires. Taps on the bar's chips work on touch.
- `zom-hud` is the score pill's phase (`DAY 2` with the clock to night, `NIGHT 3` with zombies left, `FALLEN`), the phase line under it while Tab is held (`Outpost · Day 2 · night in 0:31`, `Night 3 · 12 left`, then `Night 3 · 4 left · first light in 1:30` once the last pack is in, `Core fell · next run in 0:14`), a small panel with the scrap bank, a core health bar and the survivors left (red while the core alert shows), at night `−N tonight` in red beside the survivors once any are lost, by day a gold `Tonight · Brutes, walkers and runners from the east · 37 strong` line under it (deadliest kinds first, by `score`, and the horde's size for the squad's share), `CORE UNDER ATTACK` for 1.5s after the core loses health with an edge arrow when the core is off screen, the minimap's core, walls (sand), sentries (gold), cannons (orange), scatters (teal), mortars (violet), coils (pale blue), depots (olive), posts (mint), strips (grey) and zombie dots, `Hold E to revive <name>`, `Hold E to repair the barricade` (or `sandbag wall`, `steel wall`, `sentry`, `cannon`, `Bastion`), `Hold E to reload the sentry` (or `cannon`), with every E hint but revive hidden while the bank is empty, a `B build walls, turrets and more` and `N bring the night now` (or `ready for night · 1/2`, then `ready · 1/2 · N to wait`) hint bar by day outside build mode, the downed banner (`You're down` · `Crawl to a squadmate · 0:18` or `Being revived · 45%`), the `Squad kills` board, and kill-feed lines for downs, revives and bleed-outs. Callouts: `Night falls in 10` with tonight's forecast, `Night N` (or the night's name, `The Colossus`, `The Tide`) with the horde size and its sides, `Dawn` with the survivors lost and left and the scrap gained, then `Tonight` with the next forecast, `Back on your feet`. A bled-out player gets the death card titled `You bled out` with `The Bastion sends you back in Ns · K survivors lost` by night while enough are left, else `Back at dawn · N zombies left tonight` (just `Back at dawn` once the night is over) and no respawn button or loadout; a night joiner gets the same card titled `The night is under way`. `#report` shows the run report (`The Bastion fell on night N`, or in gold `The Bastion held. N survivors saw the morning.` after the Tide, with the score pill reading `HELD`, the run length, a table sorted by kills, then revives, then builds, and `Next run in Ns`), with a `Defense kills · Sentry 41 · Cannon 7 · Bastion 12` line when turrets or the Bastion killed anything, and while it does the canvas draws no callouts, core alert, downed banner or key hints, and the perk dock stays shut. In a run the perk dock sits above the bottom key-hint row.
- `zom-horde-path` walks the horde on a flow field out from the core. Map cover is impassable and a squad wall costs 40 cells of walking, so zombies take any open way round and chew through a wall only when the core is walled in. A building bitten to nothing ships a `boom` event and drops.
- `zom-sounds` adds synth cues in `src/client/sfx.ts`: `bite`, `splat`, `wallHit`, `wallUp`, `wallDown`, `coreHit` (once per 100 core hp), `horn`, `chime`, `downed`, `revived`, and `turret:sentry`, `turret:cannon`, `turret:scatter`, `turret:mortar` and `turret:tesla` (once per kind per snapshot; the coil's is its arc's crackle), and for building: `build:wood` (three hammer knocks), `build:sandbag` (a soft double thud), `build:steel` (a clank), `build:spikes` (a rattle) and `wallUp` for the other kinds, `upgrade` (a hammer tick and a rising chime when a building gains a level), and `aid:depot` and `aid:post` (an ammo clack and a soft chime, once per second at most). M mutes them with everything else.
- `zombies-radio` is the toy radio beside the core (`FIXED_RADIO.outpost`): the whole squad shares one station through the `radio` message (accepted only in ZOM and RNG rooms, 350 ms apart per client), and a late joiner is told the current one. By night the Bastion's own night score plays whatever the radio says, and the station returns at dawn.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the menu, scroll to Zombies, press Start a squad. Copy the invite link from the menu or the in-game chip and send it; a friend opening it lands on the menu with the squad selected and presses Play.
- By day read the gold `Tonight` line for what comes and from where, and press N when ready for it. Press B, press 1 to 9 (or click a chip) to pick a wall, sentry, cannon, scatter, mortar, tesla coil, depot, post or spike strip (press 1 again, Q or turn the wheel to change the wall's tier), point at a cell near the core and click; point at a building and press U to upgrade it. Hold E by a worn building or the worn core to repair it, by a turret short of ammo to reload it, or by a downed squadmate to revive them.

## Gotchas

- `doctor.sh` checks only the three public rooms. A squad exists only after a `POST /api/squads`.
- A squad with no human closes after 30s, bots and all, so open it and join within that window.
- `zombies.ts` needs no browser and launches none.
- The golden replay excludes zombies runs; `test/zombies-determinism.test.ts` guards their determinism instead.
- Bots revive fast, so the bled-out screen needs the fragile scratch copy and some luck; a run that logs `note revived before bleeding out` proves the downed UI but not the bled-out card.
- Night 1's forecast side is fixed by the night table (north), so `the night's zombies walk in only from the sides it forecast` checks one side; a scratch row with two sides checks more.
- Bots build only when no other human is in the squad and tend only above the reserve, so a driven browser's scrap under 260 is its own to spend; a bot squad's build plan shows only in the bench or a room with no human.
- The core alert needs zombies to reach the core. On the stock night 1 a squad often stops them first, so `zom-core-alert.png` may be missing from the default run; the fragile copy always reaches it.
- Build mode has no touch control to turn it on yet (B is out of reach on a phone), but once on, a tap on a chip of the build bar presses it.
- Stock day 1 affords one sentry after the build step's barricade and never a cannon; the cannon path needs the more-scrap scratch copy.
- A bot near a sentry reloads it far faster than it fires, so its ammo bar may sit at 10/10 all night. The turret unit tests pin the tenths, and the browser run logs how low each bar went.
